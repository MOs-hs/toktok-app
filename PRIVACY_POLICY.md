# Privacy Policy for TokTok

**Effective Date: October 2023**

TokTok ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Personal Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address provided during registration.
- **Location Data:** We collect precise location data from Drivers (Agents) to track deliveries and from Customers to provide accurate delivery estimates.
- **Photos:** Marketplace owners upload product photos to the platform.
- **Usage Data:** Information on how you interact with the app.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To facilitate the delivery process between customers, drivers, and marketplace owners.
- To maintain the "Blacklist" for safety and quality control.
- To send notifications about order status.

## 3. Data Sharing
- Customer addresses are shared with the assigned Driver.
- Driver ratings and names are visible to the Admin.
- We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 4. Security
We use Google Firebase to secure your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have questions, contact us at: support@toktok-app.com
